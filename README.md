🛍️ Myntra Sales Dashboard & Data Analysis
A sales analytics project that explores Myntra order data through key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and business recommendations.
📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes a sample Myntra sales dataset to understand product performance, customer demand, city-wise sales, payment preferences, order fulfilment, and customer ratings. The findings are presented in a dashboard-focused PowerPoint presentation with charts explained in simple language.
The dataset contains 30 orders recorded in January 2026.
🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze sales revenue and units sold across product categories.
Identify cities contributing the most orders and sales.
Compare unit sales across Women, Men, and Kids categories.
Understand order status, including delivered, returned, and cancelled orders.
Compare sales across payment methods and brands.
Review customer ratings and identify opportunities to improve customer experience.
Present insights in a clear, accessible format for business or academic use.

🔍 Key Findings
Total sales: ₹62,057 across 30 orders.
Product performance: Sneakers generated the highest sales value in this sample, at ₹7,397.
City performance: Lucknow recorded 4 orders and ₹6,595 in sales; New Delhi followed with ₹6,495 in sales.
Gender category: Women accounted for 20 units, Men for 16, and Kids for 7.
Payment method: UPI contributed the highest sales amount, at ₹23,381.
Order fulfilment: 24 orders were delivered, 4 returned, and 2 cancelled.
Customer experience: The average customer rating was 4.16 out of 5.
💡 Recommendations
Monitor stock levels for products with higher sales and unit demand.
Consider targeted promotions in cities with stronger order and sales activity.
Review return and cancellation reasons to identify fulfilment or product issues.
Keep popular payment methods, including UPI and cards, easy to use at checkout.
Compare customer ratings alongside order counts so that small samples do not distort conclusions.
Continue collecting data over a longer period to identify reliable trends and seasonality.

⚠️ Limitations
The dataset contains only 30 orders, so findings should be treated as indicative rather than representative of all Myntra customers.
The data covers a limited period and cannot establish long-term or seasonal patterns.
City-level ratings should be interpreted alongside the number of orders.
Sales amount and the sum of unit prices are different metrics; sales amount is the more appropriate measure for revenue comparisons.
The data should not be interpreted as official or comprehensive Myntra business performance unless its source and representativeness are independently verified.
📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how a small retail sales dataset can be summarized using KPIs and charts to communicate product, location, payment, fulfilment, and customer-rating insights. It provides a foundation for deeper analysis with a larger and more representative dataset.
